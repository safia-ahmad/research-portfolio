§ About

Working at the intersection of physics, retrieval, and sequential data.

I'm an undergraduate in Computer Science at Aligarh Muslim University, with an empirical machine learning background built through hands-on work on sequential model evaluation, retrieval systems, and physics-informed, time-dependent neural networks.

A theme runs through most of what I do: going past aggregate performance numbers to find the structured, systematic ways a model actually fails. That's the habit I want to carry into a PhD, valuing depth and diagnosis over a single leaderboard score.
